# Madma — Narrative Direction

> **Role:** `PLAYER_CHARACTER_NARRATIVE_DIRECTION`  
> **Status:** Project / DM direction, not in-world canon  
> **Purpose:** Long-term story architecture without inventing character facts

## Core Direction

Madma is the intended **narrative / protagonist center** of the Rolling High Club campaign.

This means the long-term story may place particular narrative weight on Madma's journey, discoveries, choices, growth, and relationship to the larger campaign.

It does **not** mean that Madma is the most powerful being, the most mechanically important character, the owner of the story, or guaranteed to determine its outcome.

The intended model is comparable to Frodo's narrative position in *The Lord of the Rings*: central to the story's journey without being greater in power or cosmic importance than the forces, artifacts, threats, and people around him.

## Guardrails

### 1. No invented destiny

Do not invent a prophecy, destiny, hidden power, ancestry implication, special status, or explanation merely because Madma is the protagonist anchor.

If the repository does not establish something, it remains unknown.

### 2. Narrative importance is not power

Narrative focus must never automatically grant:

- mechanical superiority;
- plot armor;
- automatic success;
- special authority over other Player Characters;
- immunity from consequence;
- inflated abilities or rewards.

### 3. Preserve the whole party

Madma's protagonist role must not reduce the other Player Characters to supporting cast.

Every player retains full agency, meaningful stakes, independent goals, relationships, discoveries, victories, failures, and opportunities to change the world.

### 4. Discovery over declaration

Open questions about Madma are intentional creative space.

His powers, significance, history, future hooks, relationships to larger forces, and any other currently unknown dimension should develop through:

1. real tabletop gameplay;
2. explicit brainstorming with the DM;
3. later deliberate canonization through the normal RHC persistence workflow.

Brainstorming alone is not canon.

## Authority Boundary

This file establishes **narrative direction only**.

It must never be cited as proof that an in-world fact, power, prophecy, relationship, item, ability, or historical event exists.

For factual information about Madma, use his canonical Player Character records and the normal RHC source-of-truth hierarchy.

## Future-Agent Routing

When working on long-term story architecture involving Madma, read this file before proposing protagonist-scale hooks or arcs.

Then read only the smallest relevant canonical source needed for the actual question.

Never fill an unknown merely to make the narrative direction feel complete.
