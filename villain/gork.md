## Biography

### The Orc Raid

Gork was born in a small village near Baldur’s Gate. When he was still a child, Orcs attacked the village and slaughtered the people he loved. Gork survived by hiding, powerless to protect anyone around him.

The experience became the defining trauma of his life. Gork carries a severe fear of Orcs. Their presence can bring back the helplessness of the child who watched his world collapse around him. To Gork, Orcs are not merely enemies; they are a living reminder of what it means to be defenseless.

### The Raven

As the years passed, a raven began appearing in Gork’s dreams and visions. Sometimes it simply watches him. Sometimes it seems to appear at moments when his path is uncertain.

Gork does not know what the raven is, why it appears, or whether it has any real meaning.

### The Warlock’s Offer

Eventually Gork’s path brought him to Warlock’s Crypt and to Larloch.

Larloch offered him what Gork had spent his life believing he lacked: power — the means to ensure that he would never again be helpless.

Gork accepted. He did not seek power for pleasure or destruction. He sought it because he never wanted to feel defenseless again.

### The Executioner

Over the decades that followed, Gork became powerful, disciplined, strategic, and increasingly dangerous. He became Larloch’s human instrument in the world.

Gork does not see himself as an enemy of the world. He believes that what he does serves a necessary purpose, even when his actions bring suffering to others.

His greatest lie is the justification by which he continues forward:

> **“Everything I do is necessary.”**

### The Curse

Through Larloch, Gork gained the ability to transmit an ancient necromantic curse through **Gork’s Touch**. The curse became one of his defining powers and a manifestation of the bond between servant and master.

### The Crystal Ball

During his investigations in Anauroch, Gork discovered a mysterious Crystal Ball. He does not understand its true significance.

His research continues to draw him toward ancient Netherese secrets and the search for an active Mythallar.

### Today

At 57 years old, Gork continues to serve Larloch and pursue his objectives with discipline and conviction.

Yet beneath everything he has become remains a desire that has never completely disappeared: **Gork wants to break Larloch’s curse and become truly free.**

He does not yet understand the full nature of the forces that have shaped his life.

## Psychological Profile

- Severe fear and trauma associated with Orcs.
- Does not perceive himself as evil.
- Believes power protects him from ever becoming helpless again.
- Disciplined, strategic, guarded, and internally tormented.
- Enjoys painting and drinks wine alone.
- Rarely smiles; genuine moments of happiness appear primarily while painting or when close to animals.
- **Greatest desire:** break Larloch’s curse and become truly free.
- **Greatest lie:** “Everything I do is necessary.”

## Traits

### Warlock’s Resolve
Gork has Advantage on saving throws to avoid or end the Charmed or Frightened condition on himself.

### Pact Weapon
Gork is attuned to the **Vampire**, his legendary +3 greatsword. His attacks with the Vampire are magical. The Vampire can’t be disarmed from Gork unless he is Incapacitated, as defined by the weapon’s Executioner’s Balance property.

### Blood Hunger
A successful melee hit with the Vampire grants Gork 1 Blood Stack, to a maximum of 3. Blood Stacks and all phase mechanics are resolved from `inventory/weapons/vampire.md`.

## Actions

### Multiattack
Gork makes **two attacks**, using **Vampire** or **Eldritch Blast** in any combination. He can replace **one** of these attacks with **Gork’s Touch**.

### Vampire
*Melee Attack Roll:* **+10**, reach 5 ft., one target.  
**Hit:** **14 (2d6 + 7) Slashing damage**.

The Vampire follows the normal D&D critical-hit rules. A successful melee hit also advances the Vampire’s Blood Stack / consecutive-hit system as defined in its dedicated weapon file.

### Gork’s Touch
*Melee Spell Attack Roll:* **+7**, reach 5 ft., one creature.  
**Hit:** The target is afflicted with **Larloch’s Brand** as described below.

### Eldritch Blast
*Ranged Spell Attack Roll:* **+7**, range 120 ft. Gork fires **two beams**. Each beam targets one creature.  
**Hit:** **9 (1d10 + 4) Force damage** per beam.

## Gork’s Touch — Canonical Spell

A creature struck by Gork’s Touch is afflicted with **Larloch’s Brand**, an ancient necromantic curse bestowed through Gork by Larloch.

The curse lasts until it is ended by **Rite of Renewal**, or similar magic capable of ending powerful magical curses.

**Marked by Larloch.** The point where Gork’s Touch struck begins to wither as flesh slowly recedes, exposing skeletal bone beneath. Over the next 72 hours, the corruption steadily spreads through the affected limb. This visible necromantic corruption is immediately recognized by undead. Undead have advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks made to detect the cursed creature. If an undead has multiple equally valid targets, it instinctively chooses the cursed creature first.

**Larloch’s Minor Drain.** At the end of every 24 hours while the curse remains, Larloch siphons a fragment of the victim’s life force. The cursed creature loses 4 hit points, and Larloch regains 4 hit points, even if he is on another plane of existence.

This effect ignores distance and continues until the curse is removed.

## Reaction

### Blood Parry — 3/Long Rest
When Gork is hit by a melee attack while the Vampire’s **Blood Parry** phase is active, he can use his Reaction to gain **+5 AC against that attack**, potentially causing it to miss. If the attack misses, Gork can immediately make **one Vampire attack** against the attacker as part of the same Reaction.

Resolve activation requirements and phase state from `inventory/weapons/vampire.md`.

## Vampire — Blood Release

When the Vampire’s requirements are met, Gork can consume all Blood Stacks as part of a successful melee attack. If he consumes 3 Blood Stacks, the attack deals an additional **3d8 Necrotic damage**, and Gork regains Hit Points equal to half the Necrotic damage dealt. **Cooldown:** after use, it cannot be used again until the end of Gork’s second turn.

All Vampire mechanics are controlled by the dedicated weapon reference.

## Escape Protocol

When combat turns decisively against Gork — typically when he has lost approximately half or more of his Hit Points or faces likely defeat or capture — his tactical priority becomes escape.

Gork uses **Dimension Door** or another legally available teleportation effect to withdraw. Gork is a recurring villain; ordinary combat victory does not result in his capture unless the DM explicitly establishes otherwise.

## Combat Doctrine

- Gork fights intelligently and does not waste actions proving his strength.
- At range, he pressures enemies with Eldritch Blast and uses control magic to disrupt coordinated opponents.
- In melee, he uses Vampire aggressively to build consecutive Blood Stacks and unlock its stronger phases.
- Gork’s Touch is used when placing Larloch’s Brand serves his objective; it is not merely a damage attack.
- Once Blood Release becomes available, Gork prioritizes a valuable target and uses the burst/healing effect when tactically advantageous.
- Counterspell is reserved for magic capable of disabling, trapping, or overwhelming him.
- Blood Parry is prioritized against dangerous melee attacks once its phase is active.
- Survival and continuation of his objectives take precedence over winning a single encounter.

## Balance Notes

- **Ruleset:** D&D 5.5e / 2024-style homebrew stat block
- **Target:** strong CR 7 villain
- **PB:** +3, matching CR 7
- **Skill proficiencies:** 4 — intentionally kept within the normal complexity range of official named/elite monsters rather than giving Gork player-character-style proficiency coverage
- **Baseline melee Multiattack:** 28 average Slashing damage before critical hits or Vampire escalation
- **Baseline ranged Multiattack:** up to four Eldritch Blast beams if both Multiattack choices are Eldritch Blast; the DM should normally treat each Eldritch Blast casting as one attack choice under this homebrew Multiattack
- **Burst:** Vampire’s Blood Release requires prior successful melee hits and therefore rewards sustained melee engagement rather than functioning as unconditional nova damage
- **Boss identity:** Gork’s threat comes from tactical flexibility, the Vampire’s escalation, control magic, reaction defense, and escape capability rather than inflated HP or excessive skill proficiencies
