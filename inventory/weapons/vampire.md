# Vampire

## Legendary Magic Weapon — Greatsword

> *“It does not crave blood. It remembers it.”*

**Attunement Required**

## Overview

The Vampire is a legendary greatsword bound to the will of its wielder through blood and dominance. It grows stronger as its master walks the path of the executioner.

- Two-Handed
- Heavy
- Magical

# Abilities & Passives

## Blood Hunger — Passive

The Vampire feeds on the life of your enemies.

- Each successful melee hit grants **1 Blood Stack**.
- Maximum **3 Blood Stacks**.
- Blood Stacks last until the end of the wielder’s second turn.
- Each new hit refreshes the duration.

## Executioner’s Balance — Passive

The Vampire cannot be disarmed unless the wielder is Incapacitated.

The sword remains in the wielder’s control, no matter the force.

## Blood Release — Active

As part of a successful melee attack, you can consume all Blood Stacks.

If you consume **3 Blood Stacks**:

- Deal an additional **+3d8 Necrotic damage**.
- You heal for **half the Necrotic damage dealt**.

**Cooldown:** after use, it cannot be used again until the end of the wielder’s second turn.

## Blood Parry — Reaction

When you are hit by a melee attack, you can use your reaction to activate Blood Parry.

- You gain **+5 AC against that attack**.
- If the attack misses, you can make **one melee attack against the attacker** as part of the same reaction.

**Uses:** 3 per Long Rest.

## Blood Memory — Passive / Hidden Property

Every creature killed by the Vampire leaves a memory etched within the blade.

These memories can only be uncovered through powerful magic or a specific ritual.

It grants no direct mechanical benefit.

# Combat Evolution — Consecutive Melee Hits

> **Important:** The phases below are not permanent weapon upgrades. They represent the Vampire’s escalating combat state as the wielder lands consecutive successful melee hits and accumulates Blood Stacks. Blood Stacks expire at the end of the wielder’s second turn without another successful melee hit; each new successful melee hit refreshes that duration.

## Phase I — Hunter

**Reached on the first successful melee hit / 1 Blood Stack.**

Available properties:

- Blood Hunger
- Executioner’s Balance

## Phase II — Executioner

**Reached on the second consecutive successful melee hit / 2 Blood Stacks.**

Available properties:

- Blood Hunger
- Executioner’s Balance
- Blood Release

## Phase III — Shadow of Larloch

**Reached on the third consecutive successful melee hit / 3 Blood Stacks.**

Available properties:

- Blood Hunger
- Executioner’s Balance
- Blood Release
- Blood Parry

At 3 Blood Stacks, the wielder can satisfy Blood Release’s requirement and consume all Blood Stacks as part of a successful melee attack to trigger its full effect.

## Phase IV — Unknown

**Hidden Power — Unknown.**

No mechanics are currently defined for Phase IV.

# Lore

The Vampire is an ancient blade, forged by an unknown smith in a forgotten age.

It has changed hands countless times, leaving nothing but death in its wake.

Gork found it within the Warlock’s Crypt, beside the corpse of a nameless warrior.

Since that day, the sword has never known another master.

> *A black blade that drinks deep of life and leaves only silence behind.*
